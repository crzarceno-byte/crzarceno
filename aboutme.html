MY INTERESTS!

Art has been a constant in my life but I've only taken it seriously when I picked up Digital Art.

Aside from art, video games has been an integral influence on who I am as a person. Games such as Touhou Project, Soulsgames, Disco Elysium, and other story-driven games have been important to me as I continue to grow up.

As of late, I've been getting into Tokusatsu shows. It has been a fixation of mine. Shows like Kamen Rider, Ultraman, and Kaijus like Godzilla.

Just as the same with shows, music is a type of content I also like to engage with. Musicals like Hamilton, Epic, and Falsettos, have been integral as well as my growth as a person.
